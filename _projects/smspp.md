My current research includes implementing relaxation-agnostic branch-and-bound in the [SMS++ framework](https://smspp.gitlab.io/), with a focus on mixed-integer linear programming and combinatorial optimization.

This work forms part of my PhD research at the University of Pisa with [Antonio Frangioni](https://pages.di.unipi.it/frangio/).

[View the code on GitLab](https://gitlab.com/smspp).
