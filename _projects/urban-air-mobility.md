My master's thesis at the University of Milan studied methods for traffic management in urban air mobility.

The project involves electric vertical take-off and landing aircraft (eVTOL), mixed-integer linear programming, heuristic algorithms, and combinatorial optimization.

[View the thesis code on GitHub](https://github.com/Fiden99/UAMdeconflictionMasterThesis).
