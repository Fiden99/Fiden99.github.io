I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Pisa, focusing on operations research.
I work with [Antonio Frangioni](https://pages.di.unipi.it/frangio/) on the optimization of large-scale energy systems.
My current work involves branch-and-bound and mixed-integer linear programming (MILP) within the [SMS++ framework](https://smspp.gitlab.io/).

Previously, I studied Computer Science at the University of Milan, where I worked on network flow algorithms and traffic management in urban air mobility.

You can read about my [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) and view my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).
